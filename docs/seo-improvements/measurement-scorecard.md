# Weekly SEO / AI discovery scorecard

Use the existing protected Google collector, not credentials in a browser or script arguments. Compare complete non-overlapping 28-day windows ending at least three days before collection. Keep 7-day changes secondary while samples are small.

Baseline collected 17 November: current 18 October–14 November; prior 20 September–17 October. GSC clicks 35 vs16; impressions2809 vs1511; CTR1.25% vs1.06%; averageposition35.02 vs44.51. GA4 sessions369 vs235, engagedsessions77 vs77, organicGoogle41 vs25, Bing39 vs23, UKsessions85 vs127. These are dated observations, not targets or causal release effects.

Report each week:
- GSC clicks/impressions/CTR/position by priority landing page; separate identifiable branded/nonbranded queries and note anonymised queries.
- Indexed status of improved priority pages and new report; sitemap processing, current404 withdrawals, no claimed whole-index count from a sample.
- Engaged organic sessions by landing page, UK segment separately, Google/Bing separately.
- Successful newsletter subscriptions reconciled to provider; source clicks, evidence downloads and meaningful atlas actions. No fabricated conversions from form starts.
- Known AI referral sources separately (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini), and unknown/direct kept unknown. Absence is not absence of citation.
- Engagement-time anomaly flag; do not optimise from inflated duration.
- Field CLS/LCP/INP when available; lab samples labelled with device, viewport, network and number of runs.
- Release date and collection limitations. Do not compare GSC clicks directly to GA4 sessions.

Optional manual citation panel: keep the exact same six prompts, provider, date, locale and fresh-session method each round: GB electricity mix now; GB wind generation today; notified versus metered output; GB imports; pumped storage versus hydro; dated weekly GB generation. Record cited URLs and factual errors, not only mentions. This is a sample, not a population ranking or API-backed monitoring claim.

Missing integrations: Bing Webmaster, Cloudflare verified-bot/WAF, newsletter reporting and fieldCWV require actual account evidence. Do not infer their state from GA4 or robots.txt. No new recurring schedule or paid service is enabled.
